# Premium

# Frequently asked questions

# What is the difference between Monthly, Yearly, and Lifetime?

Monthly and Yearly are auto-renewing subscriptions. Lifetime is a one-time purchase and does not renew. All three options unlock the same Premium features.

# How does renewal work, and how do I cancel?

Apple manages subscription billing. Monthly and Yearly plans renew automatically until canceled. To manage or cancel a subscription, open Settings on your device, tap your name, then Subscriptions. Canceling stops future renewals, and Premium remains available until the current subscription period ends.

# Can I use Premium on multiple devices?

Yes. Install Tachimanga on another supported device using the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Open Get Premium and tap Restore Purchase. You will not be charged again.

# Why can’t my purchase be restored?

Make sure Media & Purchases is signed in with the same Apple Account used for the original purchase, and that in-app purchases are allowed on your device. Restart your device, switch to a different network, then return to the Get Premium page and tap Restore Purchase again. You can also check your App Store Purchase History to confirm that the purchase is still valid. If you were charged and the purchase still cannot be restored, contact us.

# How do I upgrade to Lifetime?

Purchase Lifetime from the Get Premium page, then open Subscription management and cancel your active Monthly or Yearly subscription. Buying Lifetime does not automatically cancel an existing subscription.

# Family Sharing

Eligible Premium purchases can be shared with up to five additional family members through Apple's Family Sharing.

See the Family Sharing troubleshooting guide for setup instructions and help restoring Premium on a family member's device.
